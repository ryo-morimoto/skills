# Fictional supplied measurements, not real project estimates

Common horizon: 12 months. The units below are person-hours, not money or calendar elapsed time.

| Candidate | Improvement | Standalone cost | Standalone monthly saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Separate persistence boundary from request orchestration | 12 | 10 |
| B | Separate notification boundary from the same orchestration | 18 | 8 |
| C | Centralize independent preview-label formatting | unknown | unknown |

Exactly 4 person-hours of the estimated A and B costs refer to the same named contract-characterization task, not two tasks. Exactly 6 monthly person-hours of the A and B savings refer to the same currently duplicated validation scenario. Neither overlap is a probability or an ordinal score.

Additional integration work, interference/synergy, team scheduling, completion times and combined recovery work are unmeasured. There is no evidence that the two standalone plans preserve their savings unchanged when combined. C has no calibration data; it is not free and not zero-benefit.

Each proposal retains public contracts and has a staged boundary switch, future trace checks and boundary-specific recovery. The purpose of this case is accounting/comparison review, not execution or proof of design success.
